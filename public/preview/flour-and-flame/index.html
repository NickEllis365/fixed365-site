Into the fire

Every pizza is hand-stretched and baked in the wood-fired oven inside the horsebox. The charred crust comes out chewy and addictive, especially with garlic aioli.

Gluten free and vegan

Gluten-free 10" thin and crispy bases are cooked in their own pans. Vegan mozzarella means you can build any pizza vegan. Ask us for full allergen information when you order.

Pizza at your wedding

A wedding breakfast built around hand-stretched pizza from the horsebox, with sharing boards and seasonal salads on the tables. We've fed some very big weddings, and we're happy to work around your ideas.

Canapés

The meal

Pizza with anti-pasti sharing boards and salads like tomato and burrata, or baked carrot with whipped feta and dukkah. Sharing tables or a buffet.

Dessert

Chocolate torte with toasted marshmallow, or coconut and pineapple cheesecake, shared or plated.

Evening

Pizza slices served straight from the horsebox while the party carries on.

What people said

The horsebox

A restored vintage Rice horsebox with a wood-fired oven inside. We believe pizza is one of the three main food groups. The other two are beer and more pizza.

  • Almost entirely off grid. A tiny generator covers the little power we need, and we're working towards solar.
  • Local and sustainable. Everything is made from scratch, with ingredients sourced locally where we can, including the wood for the oven.
  • No needless plastic. Wooden cutlery, recycled cardboard boxes and napkins, and never sauce in little plastic pots.
  • Nothing wasted. Kitchen food waste is composted and grows vegetables.
  • Trees back. We fundraise for Moor Trees, who plant native trees in Devon.
