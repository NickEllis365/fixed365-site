Loft conversions

When it comes to creating extra space in your home, a loft conversion can be the most cost-effective way of doing it. It uses your building's existing roof structure, which brings the cost down a long way compared with other conversions and extensions.

It's also a chance to add some individuality to your home. Whether it's a games room for the kids or an extra bedroom for a growing family, we build it for the way you'll use it.

A loft conversion could add as much as 20% to the value of your home.

Cut roofs

Cut roofs with traditional rafters leave more space underneath. They can be used to create cathedral ceilings or storage within the rafters, and they're easier to alter later on.

Whether trusses or a traditional cut roof suit you depends on the ceiling and roof you want, and there are many styles to choose from. We've built a lot of cut roofs and can talk it through with you to find the best solution.

Thinking about living in the attic one day? A cut roof now will save you time and money later.

Extensions

No other way of adding space gives you quite as much scope. Depending on the land you have, an extension can increase your home substantially and give you a living space built to your exact requirements.

It's far easier than going through the stress of moving, and far easier than buying a plot and building from scratch. It's also your chance to put your own signature on your home.

  • Study or home office
  • Playroom
  • Ground floor bedroom
  • Dining room
  • Annex for a relative
  • Homework space

Garage conversions

More and more people are turning the garage from somewhere to keep the car into another useful room.

The most common use is a study, whether that's for a parent working from home or a dedicated place for the children to do their homework. Other popular choices are a playroom, a ground floor bedroom, a separate dining room (especially if you have a lounge-diner) or an annex for an elderly relative.

Kitchen installations

The kitchen is the hub of most homes. It's where everyone gathers and where most of the day happens, so it makes sense to have it exactly the way you want it.

We'll make sure your kitchen is installed to a high professional standard.
