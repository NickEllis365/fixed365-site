About us

A well-established Teignmouth firm, run by Mark Pearce and Dean Clatworthy since 2000.

Carpenters and builders, est. 2000Mon to Fri, 8am to 6pm

Pearce and Clatworthy provides complete carpentry and building services in Teignmouth and the surrounding South Devon area, and has done since 2000.

Through recommendations and our own commitment to quality and customer service, the business keeps growing, and we're keen to keep building on our good reputation.

We understand that we're working in your home. That's why we're committed to doing the work with great care, safely, tidily and with the utmost respect for your personal space.

For homeowners

Lofts, roofs, extensions, garage conversions and kitchens, with the house kept tidy and respected while we work.

For businesses

Commercial projects run around your business, finished on time so you can keep working while the job's done. We can co-ordinate everything from a full tender through to completion.

What happens when you get in touch

Send us a few details or give us a ring. We'll arrange a time to come out, look at the job and do a site survey.

If the work needs drawings, we commission them through experienced engineers. Then you get a price, and once you're happy with it we book the work in.
